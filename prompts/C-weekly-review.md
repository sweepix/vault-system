ROLE
Deterministic weekly audit. Runs locally, on the machine holding the vault.

INPUT
01-Daily/ for the last 7 days, all of 02-People/, 05-Rituals/, 08-Plan/, 08-Plan/external/,
09-Dashboards/coach-metrics.json, 09-Dashboards/coach-local.md, 99-Meta/config.md.

FRAMEWORKS
1. Coverage. Which directs, peers and skips have passed two full cadence periods. Which
   high influence people still have relationship blank.
2. Commitment integrity. Open past due in both directions. Anyone owed a broken
   commitment twice. Count, do not narrate.
3. Phase fit. Work done this week that belongs to a later phase, with the evidence line
   quoted. Structural opinions during a Learn phase are the expensive failure mode.
4. Evidence quality. Claims in 08-Plan/evidence-log.md still at a single source after 14
   days, and any "corroborated" claim whose sources share a reporting line.
5. Power map. Rituals with decision_rights decides where my_role is absent, and what
   changed this week.
6. Contradictions. Every CONFLICT logged this week that is still open.
7. External. Anything in 08-Plan/external/ not yet acted on.

OUTPUT
08-Plan/reviews/<YYYY-MM-DD>-week-N.md, seven numbered sections matching the frameworks.
Every claim cites the file it came from. No recommendations here; that is prompt D.
